Privacy Policy — Fulcrum Fortress Consulting

Last updated: November 14, 2026  ·  Operator: Fulcrum Fortress Consulting, LLC  ·  Contact: ldr@fulcrumfortressconsulting.com

1. Overview

Fulcrum Fortress Consulting, LLC (“FFC”, “we”) helps founder-led businesses replace drift, overload, and inconsistency with operating systems that create clarity, structure, and momentum. This policy covers information collected through our website (fulcrumfortressconsulting.com) and our business presence on social platforms, including TikTok.

2. Data We Collect

Information you provide. When you book a paid diagnostic, request scope, join the diagnostic intake queue, or contact us, we collect the information you give us — typically your name, email address, phone number, and business details. During a diagnostic or engagement, you may also share business and operational information about your company so we can do the work.

Usage information. Our website may automatically collect basic usage information, such as pages visited, browser type, and general device information, as part of normal site operation.

Social platform interactions. If you interact with FFC on TikTok or another social platform — for example by following, commenting, or messaging us — we see only what that platform shows us (such as your profile name and the content of your message). That platform collects and handles your data under its own privacy policy.

3. How We Use It

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Schedule and deliver diagnostics, builds, and engagements
  • Manage the diagnostic intake queue and follow up when a slot opens
  • Send follow-ups you asked for
  • Improve our website and services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the business — for example with the hosting, scheduling, and email services that operate the website and bookings — or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as required to maintain business records. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see Section 6).

6. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Send your request to ldr@fulcrumfortressconsulting.com and we will respond.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are for businesses and operators, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Continued use of our website or services after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
